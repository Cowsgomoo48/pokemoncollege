# v0.96.0: Tower maps, rooms, Merchant, relics and card upgrades

## Battle screen
- **Enemy statuses:** the turn count is a small subscript number on the icon. Intents are unchanged.
- **Ability cards:** an Ability card you've played now shows as a small card just above END TURN. Hover over it on desktop or tap it to see the full card. The old ✨ chips are gone.
- **Planted cards:** planted cards appear in a column on the right showing their art, with no text overlay.
  - Each one has a 🌱 counter badge.
  - The border pulses green until you Harvest it that turn.
  - Tap one to enlarge it and use HARVEST or REAP.
  - Constructs and Silk Cocoons use the same column.
- **Map button:** a 🗺️ button sits next to the 🗼 floor label. Your relics show under the top bar.

## Branching tower maps
- Each environment is a tower with a branching map. Its length depends on the **Tower length** you pick when starting a run:
  - Easy: 8–10 floors
  - Balanced: 10–12 floors
  - Hard: 8–16 floors
- **Map layout:**
  - The first floor is all battles.
  - The floor below the boss is all Rest Sites.
  - Every tower has at least one Merchant around the middle.
  - Each room links to 1–2 rooms on the next floor, and every room can be reached.
- After each room you pick the next one on the map. The climb animation then names the room you're heading to.
- Beating the boss starts a new tower in the next environment.

## Room types
- **⚔️ Battle / 👑 Boss:** card fights, as before.
- **💎 Treasure Room:** answer 2–3 questions. Each right answer opens a chest, which can hold:
  - a card of your choice
  - 6–10 Tickets
  - a PokeBox, sent to your Bag
  - a free card upgrade

  If you answer all 3 of 3 right, you also get a GreaterBox or, sometimes, a Relic.
- **❓ ? Room:** 30% of the time it's an ambush battle. Otherwise it's one of these events:
  - Mysterious Shrine
  - Lost Pack
  - Wishing Well
  - Hot Spring
  - Strange Mirror (duplicate a card)
  - Transmutation Pool (transform a card)
  - Quiz Master
- **🔥 Rest Site:** heal 25% of Max HP, or upgrade a card.
- **🛒 Merchant:**
  - The merchant sits on the rug in the middle of the shop scene.
  - **Cards:** 6 for sale, picked from your team's types plus shared cards, at 7–14 Tickets. Sometimes one is half price.
  - **Relics:** 3 for sale, at 20–28 Tickets.
  - **Card Removal Service:** 10 Tickets, plus 1 per 2 floors climbed and 4 more for each card you've already removed. Once per visit.

## Card upgrades (Card+)
- Every card can be upgraded at a Rest Site, and Treasure Rooms can upgrade one at random.
- **What an upgrade does:** cards with damage, Block or healing numbers get those numbers raised by 30%, rounded up. Other cards cost 1 less, or gain Retain if they already cost 0.
- An upgrade shows as "Name+" and a green ＋ badge. Before you upgrade, you see the card now and after.
- These upgrades follow a general rule. Individual cards can be given their own upgrade if you want to design specific ones.
