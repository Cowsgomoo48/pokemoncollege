# v0.96.1: Tower map as the tower itself
- The map is now drawn as the tower: one wide floor slab per floor, in the current environment, with that floor's rooms as large round icons. Dashed lines between floors show which rooms connect; the path you took is drawn in blue.
- The current floor is outlined and your team stands on its room. Tap a glowing room to climb: the view scrolls up, the team walks to the room, and a banner names the room (and the environment on a tower's first floor).
- The 🗺️ button opens the same tower view.
- New crossed-swords icon for Battle rooms on the map and in the climb, replacing the blurry ⚔️ emoji icon.
