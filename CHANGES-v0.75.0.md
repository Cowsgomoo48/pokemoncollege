# v0.75.0
- Dungeon/Tower team picker follows the Box sort order.
- **Mass Release**: long-press a Pokémon in the Box. Checkboxes appear, Pokémon shudder (high-friendship ones sweat). CONFIRM shows a 3-column list, the count, and the total Cogs/Poképuffs, with RELEASE ALL / CANCEL. EXIT clears everything. Pokémon on runs or breeding can't be selected.
- **Mass delete for Summaries**: long-press a summary, tap others, then DELETE.
- Gender symbols: always shown (no longer cut off by long names), larger, and forced to text style on iOS.
