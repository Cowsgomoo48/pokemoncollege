# PokémonCollege v0.34.0

## New items (from boxes, and sometimes Daily Deals)
- **Costume Ticket** (Rare): puts a Pokémon that has a costume variant into it. That's Ralts, Gardevoir, Charcadet, Armarouge, Ceruledge, Snorunt and Froslass.
- **Reroll Ticket** (Uncommon): one free Daily Deals reroll. It stays in your bag until used.
- **Shiny Ticket** (Legendary): turns one non-shiny Pokémon shiny. The old "Shiny Ticket" (3 Safaris with triple shiny odds) is now called **Shiny Lure**.
- **Copper Bottlecap**: +1 grade to one chosen stat's growth rate.
- **Silver Bottlecap**: +1 grade to every growth rate.
- **Golden Bottlecap**: +2 grades to every growth rate.
- **Stellar Bottlecap** (incredibly rare): every growth rate becomes A+.
- **EXP Tickets** (+25% for 3 days, +50% for 5 days, +75% for 7 days): different tickets stack. Using one that's already running restarts its timer. Active tickets show as icons by your level; tap one to see the time left.

## Weights
- **Boxes:** every box prize has a weight within its tier, so valuable prizes come up less. A Costume Ticket is far more common than a costumed Pokémon.
- **Daily Deals:** rarer items cost more, up to 4× the base price.

## Daily Deals rerolls
- **Cost:** two free rerolls a day, then 10 Cogs, doubling each time (10 → 20 → 40 → 80 …). Resets every day.
- **Reroll Tickets:** these give extra free rerolls on top.

## Evolution
- **Moves:** a Pokémon keeps its moves when it evolves, unless the evolved form's best move for that slot is stronger. In that case it learns the stronger one.
- **Ultimates:** Ultimates carry over (Meltan → Melmetal keeps it).
