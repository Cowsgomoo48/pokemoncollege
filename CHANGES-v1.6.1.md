# v1.6.1

- **New card: Revolutionize** (Steel, 1 AP, Construct 1). Attach one of your Planted cards (one that has a Harvest) as its component.
  - The attached card keeps its current Plant counters but can't gain more, so pump it with Fertilize or Overgrowth before you play this.
  - At the end of your turn, it activates the component's Harvest twice, then removes 1 counter.
  - When it runs out, both cards are discarded.
  - Its Construct tile shows the counters left (♻️ ⚙️1·🌱N).
  - Can't be played without a Planted card that has a Harvest.
- Synergy tests: 63 checks.
- Service worker cache bumped to 1042.
