# PokemonCollege v0.62.8

## Difficulty: max damage per foe hit
| | Easy | Balanced | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 1.2× | 0.55× | 0.5× |
| Now | 0.5× | 0.65× | 1.2× |

Easy is now the most forgiving and Hard the harshest. This applies to both Mystery Dungeon and the classic dungeon.

## Mystery Dungeon boss arenas
- **Bigger boss room:** boss floors carve one large arena toward the far side of the map (about 3–4× the size of a normal room on average). The boss waits on the hidden stairs in the middle.
- **Bosses wait:** the boss and everything else in its room sit idle. They can't wake up, attack, be targeted or join a battle until **every living teammate** is inside the room. Fainted teammates don't need to enter.
- **Sealing:** once your whole team is in, a **BOSS / TOTEM / ALPHA BATTLE** splash shows the boss's name, every doorway into the room is walled off with **red force fields**, and the fight starts.
- **Boss defeated:** the force fields go down and the stairs appear.
