# PokémonCollege v0.39.0

## TMs
- **Used up again:** teaching a TM uses up the disc. It still costs no Cogs: tap Teach and you're done.
- **183 new TMs** from your list. Legendary and Mythical signature moves were left out, and so was Light of Ruin (Eternal Floette's move).
- **Stats:** each new move's type, power and Physical/Special category come from the main games, scaled to this game's numbers.
- **Who can learn them:** learnsets were rebuilt from the main games, including moves a Pokémon's pre-evolutions learn. Mew can learn all 294 moves.
- **Move rules shown:** the TM screen and the Teach box both say what a move's special rule is.

## Multi-hit moves
- **Moves:** Bullet Seed, Rock Blast, Arm Thrust, Pin Missile, Icicle Spear, Bone Rush, Scale Shot, Water Shuriken, Barrage, Comet Punch, Double Hit, Dual Chop, Dual Wingbeat, Twin Beam, Twineedle, Bonemerang, Triple Dive, Triple Kick, Triple Axel and Beat Up.
- **Power is per hit.** It's listed as, for example, "3 pw ×2–5".
- **How many hits:** 2–5-hit moves land 2 or 3 hits most of the time (35% each) and 4 or 5 hits less often (15% each). The others always hit the same number of times.
- **Rising hits:** Triple Kick and Triple Axel get stronger with each hit.
- **Beat Up:** hits once for each teammate still standing.
- **Each hit counts separately:** type matchups apply to every hit and each hit shows its own damage number, so super-effective multi-hit moves pull ahead.
- **Enemies too:** wild Pokémon using these moves hit multiple times as well.

## Moves with their own rules
- **Your examples:**
  - **Trump Card:** +20 power each time it's used in a battle.
  - **Rage Fist:** +10 power per fainted teammate.
  - **Revenge:** double power while a teammate is fainted, with a red glow on the button.
  - **Return:** stronger the more of your Box has spent less time as your main Pokémon than this one.
  - **Frustration:** stronger the more of your Box has spent more time as your main Pokémon than this one.
- **Teammates fainting:**
  - **Last Respects:** +50 power per fainted teammate.
  - **Retaliate:** double power if a teammate fainted during the last enemy turn.
- **Getting hit:**
  - **Payback / Avalanche:** double power if the user was hit during the last enemy turn.
  - **Metal Burst:** deals 1.5× the damage the user just took.
  - **Shell Trap:** only works right after being hit.
  - **Focus Punch:** fails if the user was hit.
- **Using a move repeatedly:**
  - **Fury Cutter / Rollout / Ice Ball:** power doubles with each use in a row, up to ×4.
  - **Gigaton Hammer:** can't be used twice in a row.
  - **Stomping Tantrum:** double power after a failed move.
  - **First Impression:** only works as the user's first attack of a battle.
- **Comparing stats with the target:**
  - **Heavy Slam / Heat Crash:** power depends on how much heavier the user is, using real Pokédex weights.
  - **Gyro Ball:** stronger the faster the target is.
  - **Shell Side Arm:** uses whichever of Attack or Sp. Atk does more damage.
- **HP:**
  - **Eruption:** weakens as the user loses HP.
  - **Wring Out:** weakens as the target loses HP.
  - **Brine:** double power at half HP or below.
- **Target status:**
  - **Hex / Venoshock / Barb Barrage:** double power on a target with a status.
  - **Smelling Salts / Wake-Up Slap:** double power on a paralyzed target, but cure it.
  - **Salt Cure:** salts the target, which loses HP every round. It loses more if it's Water or Steel, and less if it's a boss.
- **Fixed damage:** Dragon Rage always deals ¼ of the user's max HP. Psywave deals a random 10–40%.
- **Stamina:** Spit Up spends all your Stamina for more power.
- **Type tricks:** Freeze-Dry is super effective on Water. Flying Press counts as both Fighting and Flying. Synchronoise only hits foes that share a type with the user.
- **Always critical:** Storm Throw and Frost Breath.
- **Self-damage:** Steel Beam and Chloroblast cost half the user's max HP.
- **Draining moves:** Leech Life, Draining Kiss, Bitter Blade, Parabolic Charge and Matcha Gotcha heal the user.
- **Conditional power:** Fickle Beam sometimes fires all-out for double power. Fishious Rend does double damage to a target that hasn't attacked yet. Stored Power triples with a Starf boost. Acrobatics doubles when the run bag is empty.
- **Double Shock:** Electric types only, and once per battle.
- **Red glow:** any of these moves shows the red glow on its battle button while its bonus is active. A button is greyed out, with the reason, when its move can't be used right now.
