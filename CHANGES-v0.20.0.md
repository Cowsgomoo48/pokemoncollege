# v0.20.0: Magearna comes to life
- New shop scene from your art: the empty shop as the background, with your Magearna standing behind the counter as her own layer (the counter and bell sit in front of her).
- Her arms are separate pieces that pivot at the gold shoulder rings. She rests with her arms down and bobs gently while idle.
- Moods, each with its own cry from your recording:
  - Standard: waves hello when you enter the shop, waves goodbye when you leave, squishes when tapped.
  - Curious: sometimes when tapped (head tilt), and when you use the free Daily Deals refresh.
  - Happy: cheers and hops when you buy anything (Daily Deals, boxes, Rare Candy, Poképuffs).
  - Sad: droops when you can't afford something.
  - Mad: shakes with her fists up if you poke her 5 times within 3 seconds.
- Settings → "Magearna's voice" turns her cries on or off (on by default). Everything still animates when it's off.
- The new art and the five cries are saved for offline use.
