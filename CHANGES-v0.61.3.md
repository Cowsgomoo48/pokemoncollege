# PokémonCollege v0.61.3

## 🟣 Soul Stones live in the Bag
- **Where they go:** Soul Stones earned at the end of a Mystery Dungeon run go into your 🎒 Bag, under **Tickets & Boxes**.
- **Spending them:** the Death Deals take them straight from the Bag. Tap the Soul Stone in the Bag for **SPEND AT THE DEATH DEALS**.
- **Old Soul Stones:** any you already had are moved into the Bag automatically.
