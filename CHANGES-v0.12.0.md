# v0.11–0.12: Shop, boxes, bag, habitat
Pass 1 (0.11)
- Growth letters: two-dice style roll, so middling grades are common and A-range grades are rare (A+ ≈1% per stat; all six B- or better ≈1 in 375); a perfect all-A+ set is its own 1-in-4096 roll. New Pokémon store their growth; old ones keep a seeded roll under the new odds.
- Safari: Common Costumes 1/496, Rare Costumes 1/2048 (only for species that have a custom costume sheet — none yet). A ball sparkles if its Pokémon is Rare or rarer, shiny, costumed, or has every growth letter above C+.
- Shop: Magearna's counter (your picture), squish + chirp on tap, hello on entering and goodbye on leaving. 9 Daily Deals on wooden shelves (new set every 6 hours, one free refresh per day): items, Cog TMs, Cog boxes (50/100/150), rarely a Pokémon. Box Shop: PokeBox 5, GreaterBox 10, UltraBox 15 Poképuffs with your odds and item-count rerolls, and the twirl-up/plop/shadow/explode opening animation.
- Bag: swipe up from the bottom bar (or tap the 🎒 tab). Tabs, item details, Use/Open/Feed/Place.
- Snacks give a timed companion-EXP multiplier that keeps counting down when the Pokémon isn't your main; buffed Pokémon get rising blue particles in the pasture and the Box.
- Shiny Ticket: triples shiny chance for the next 3 Safaris and stacks. Safari Tickets in the bag give a free pull.
Pass 2 (0.12)
- Double-tap the habitat to enlarge it; a tray shows your snacks (tap to feed) and toys (tap to toss), plus EDIT.
- Edit tabs: Small Decor (drag anywhere, drag off to put away), Medium Decor (4 spots; dropping on a used spot sends the old one back), Backgrounds (47, cut from your images; the old one, including the default pasture, goes back to the bag).
- Feeding Bowl: the Pokémon walks over and eats. Training Dummy: it walks over and attacks (the dummy wobbles). Cozy Bed: at night (10 PM–7 AM) it sleeps there and the habitat darkens; tapping the habitat or opening the app wakes it, and it goes back to sleep after a minute alone.
- Bundled PMD Eat/Attack/Sleep animations (Eat exists for only 24 species; others use Idle).
- 0.12.1: First Rare Costume: Gardevoir 'Midnight Coat' (1/2048 when a Safari ball holds Gardevoir), using the sprite the user supplied as a single pose (mirrored and bobbing while it walks; shown in the Box and stat sheet instead of the portrait).
