# PokémonCollege v0.55.0

## ✍️ Short answers
- **What changed:** about 1 question in 5 now asks you to **type** the answer instead of picking it. These show a purple ✍️ TYPE IT badge.
- **Which questions:** practice problems (type the result), terms (type the term that matches a definition), and quiz questions whose answer is three words or fewer.
- **Checking is fair, not picky:**
  - Capitals and spaces don't matter.
  - "4" counts for "x = 4".
  - You can leave off units ("40" for "40 Ω").
  - Numbers within 1% count, since answers are rounded.
  - Any equivalent algebra counts ("50x − 50" for "10(5x − 5)").
  - A small typo in a word is fine.
  - A wrong value or the wrong sign never passes. In a check of 448 generated problems, no wrong answer was accepted.
- **Typing help:** a row of maths keys (x, ², ^, √, π, ×, ÷ …) plus the calculator, with **↳ Use this result** to drop the calculator's answer into your box.
- **If you miss:** you see the answer and the worked solution, then a multiple-choice Make-Up, as before.

## 🛠 Do It Yourself
- **What it is:** on some floors the first attack question is a practice problem you work out **line by line**, like on paper.
- **Building your lines:** add as many lines as you need. Type or tap maths keys into a line, and use **↳ Add to my line** to drop the calculator's working and result into it. Your last line should end with the answer.
- **CHECK MY WORK** then shows:
  - ✓ on every line whose maths adds up, and ✗ with what went wrong ("Left side is 28, but the right side is 29"). Steps with x are checked against the real answer when it's an "x = …" problem.
  - Whether your final answer is right.
  - Your work next to the worked solution, so you can compare how the steps are written.
  - **💭 Ask the tutor where I went wrong**, with your Gemini key: the AI points to your first slip and gives one tip for writing steps more clearly.
- **When it shows up:**
  - Battle Tower: every boss floor and every 3rd floor.
  - Mystery Dungeon: every boss floor and about half of the other floors.
  - Only for study sets that have practice problems.
- **Reward:** solving it counts as a perfect answer (the easiest timing bar) and gives 5 bonus 🎟️ Tickets.

## 🤔 Confidence tap
- **What it is:** after a first-try correct answer you're asked **"Did you know it?"**: ✅ Knew it or 🤔 Guessed.
- **What it does:** a guess still wins your turn, but that question comes back later as a weak spot.
- **If you don't tap:** after 6 seconds it counts as "Knew it".
- **When it's skipped:** typed answers (typing proves you knew it) and Make-Ups.
- **In the export:** Export Study Data now has a **Guessed** column, and guessed items show up under "Struggling with".

## 📋 Mistake recap
- **End of run:** when a Mystery Dungeon or Battle Tower run ends, the end screen lists what you missed: the question, what you picked, the right answer and one line of explanation (the 15 most recent).
- **Mid-run:** **⚙ Run settings → 📋 Mistakes this run**.
