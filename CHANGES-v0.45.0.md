# PokémonCollege v0.45.0

## TM Counter rerolls
- **Free reroll:** the TM Counter (Shop) has one free reroll a day, under the TMs.
- **Reroll Tickets:** they can now reroll the TM Counter as well as the Daily Deals. Use one from the TM Counter button or from the ticket in your bag.
- **Fresh stock:** a reroll restocks the TM Counter, so TMs you'd already bought today can be bought again from the new stock.

## Dungeon Tickets 🎟️
- **What changed:** Mystery Dungeon runs now pay 🎟️ Tickets instead of Cogs, at the same rates (battles 3+, swarms 5+, bosses 12–18+, and treasure rooms).
- **Spending:** the dungeon shop is priced in Tickets.
- **Run end:** when a run ends (cleared, lost or ended early), leftover Tickets turn into Cogs 1:1. The floor-clear bonus is still paid in Cogs.
- **Run summary:** the summary at the end shows how many Tickets you earned, how many were left over, and the Cogs you got.

## Cheaper evolutions
- **New costs:** evolution costs are down by about 60%. 25 → 10, 50 → 20 and 100 → 40 Rare Candies. Magikarp and the other 400-candy evolutions now cost 150.

## Slightly more Rare Candy
- **Tasks:** a Rare Candy comes every 6 completed tasks instead of every 8.
- **Minigames:** you get grade ÷ 28 Rare Candy instead of grade ÷ 33.
- **Box candy prizes:** come up about 20–25% more often (×1: 0.8 → 1, ×3: 0.7 → 0.85, ×8: 0.5 → 0.6).
