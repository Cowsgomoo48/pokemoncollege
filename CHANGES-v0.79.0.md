# v0.79.0 — Living dungeons
- Walls: textured per layer (rock, spiked stalagmite, brick, basalt) with taller raised front faces; moss drips, sulfur crust, lava veins.
- Floor dressing per layer: puddles, moss, cracks, coal, sulfur stains, ash, gold flecks.
- Special rooms (about half of floors): pond (green acid pond in Bubbling Caverns), mossy garden with flowers and bushes, cracked lava floor with glowing cracks (Bubbling/Molten/Core). Visual only; ponds never hold the stairs.
- Ambient motion (CSS, light): wall torches/shell lamps with flicker and glow, water drips, drifting spores/dust/bubbles/steam/embers/sparks, pulsing crystals, pond glints, garden butterflies, pulsing lava cracks. Respects Reduce Motion.
- **Ruined houses** (about 30% of non-boss floors): a big room with real inner walls and doorways, wooden plank and tiled floors, timber-framed walls with windows, and walk-over debris (crates, planks, rubble, couch, shelves, broken glass). Always fully connected; no obstacles spawn inside.
