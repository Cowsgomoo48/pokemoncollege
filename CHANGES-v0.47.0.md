# PokémonCollege v0.47.0

## 🏰 Mystery Dungeon is now played on tile floors
- **Replaces the room map:** each floor is a top-down tile map with rooms joined by corridors. You explore it with your whole team, and wild Pokémon wait in the rooms. You can only see the room you're in plus a couple of tiles around each teammate, and the minimap in the corner fills in as you explore.
- **Your turn:** you control every Pokémon on your team. Tap a teammate (on the map or on the chips under it) to pick it, then:
  - **👣 Move (optional, once a turn):** tap a blue tile. How far a Pokémon can go depends on its species' Speed: 2 tiles under 50 Speed, 3 under 80, 4 under 110, and 5 for the fastest. Foes block the way, and you can walk past teammates.
  - **Then one action:** ⚔️ Fight, 🎒 Item or 🛡 Guard. Only actions ask a study question, so moving is free.
  - **⏭ End Turn:** whenever you're done. The enemies move once everyone has acted.
- **Where you stand matters:** every move now has a reach, shown on its button.
  - Fast physical moves: the 8 tiles next to you.
  - Fast special moves: a straight line, 3 tiles.
  - Charged physical moves: a lunge, 2 tiles.
  - Charged special moves: a line of 4 tiles. Beams (Thunderbolt, Hydro Pump, Flamethrower, Hyper Beam and similar) reach 5 tiles and hit every foe in the line.
  - Spread moves: every foe within 2 tiles.
  - Lines go in all 8 directions and stop at walls.
  - Ultimates hit foes within 3 tiles (all-foe Ultimates) or one foe within 5.
- **Wild Pokémon:** they sleep (💤) until your team comes close, then hunt you down and attack with the same reach rules. Some rooms hold a swarm of weak Pokémon.
- **Stairs 🪜:** step on them to go down. Every floor heals your team 20% and gives +10 Stamina. Your team also slowly recovers HP while nothing is chasing it.
- **Bosses:** the last floor of each stretch has a boss guarding the stairs: 3 floors on Easy, 4 on Balanced and 5 on Hard. Totems and Alphas still appear on Balanced and Hard. A boss sometimes charges a blast that hits everyone within 3 tiles, so Guard or get away. Beat it and the stairs appear; then leave with the boss bonus or go deeper.
- **On the ground:** items and 🎟️ Ticket piles to pick up by walking over them, plus the occasional 💎 Treasure Chest, ⛲ Healing Spring or 🛒 Kecleon Shop.
- **Rewards:** each foe you beat gives Tickets and team EXP right away (bosses give more, plus a berry, and Totems and Alphas still give you their pre-evolution).
- **Looks:** five tile themes that change with each boss stretch: Grassy Plains, Rocky Cave, Frosty Grotto, Ember Caverns and Crystal Depths. They're drawn by the app, not taken from the games.
- **Items:** items that hit "every enemy" (Stellar Storm, Pocket Voltorb, Thundurus’ Might) hit every foe you can see. The Snag Machine works on any foe within 4 tiles.
- **Runs in progress:** a run saved in the old room-map version continues on a fresh tile floor with the same team, bag and Tickets.

## Kept
- Questions, the timing slider, items, Guard, Revive, Ultimates, Tickets, difficulties and Casual/Videogamey styles all work as before.
