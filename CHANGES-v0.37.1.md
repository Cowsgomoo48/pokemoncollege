# PokémonCollege v0.37.1 — dungeon levels
- **Normal encounters:** levels spread around your team: most are close, some run well below and some above (about 6 under to 3 over the level cap).
- **Swarms:** now 3–10 levels under your team, about 6 under on average.
- **Totem allies:** spread around 2 under your team.
- **Bosses, Totems and Alphas:** vary by a level or two.
