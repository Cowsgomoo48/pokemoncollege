# PokémonCollege v0.61.0

## ⛏ Dig spots (Mystery Dungeon)
- **Finding them:** faint sparkles on the floor, easy to miss at first. The Shinier Patches upgrade makes them easier to spot. Each floor has 1–2 of them, more with the Prospector upgrade.
- **Digging:** walk onto one while no wild Pokémon is awake in sight and a dig starts. Each spot can be dug 2–5 times, and each question is harder and pays better:
  1. A normal question from your set (multiple choice): 3–5 🎟️, sometimes a stone.
  2. A harder question (multiple choice), taken from the ones you miss most: 5–8 🎟️ + 1 stone.
  3. A typed answer: Tickets, 1–2 stones, maybe an item.
  4. A typed answer from your weak spots: Tickets, 2 stones and an item.
  5. A typed answer from a hard question you often get wrong: Tickets, one of each stone, a better item and **1 🟣 Soul Stone**.
- **Exit Dig:** from the second question on, an **EXIT DIG** button under the question lets you stop and keep what you found.
- **Wrong answers:** a wrong answer caves the spot in (you keep what you already found). A Make-Up answer halves that dig's reward.

## 🟠🔵🟢 Stones
- **🟠 Fire Stones** pay for offense upgrades, **🔵 Water Stones** for defense, and **🟢 Leaf Stones** for utility.
- **Where they come from:** you find them by digging. Beaten foes also drop them sometimes (20% each, less for swarm Pokémon, and 2 from every boss).

## 🎩 Hatterene's Upgrade Shop (replaces the Kecleon Shop in Mystery Dungeon)
- **Opening it:** it's a full screen like Magearna's shop. Step on the 🎩 spot on a floor (it shows up more often than the Kecleon Shop did), or tap **HATTERENE'S SHOP** after beating a boss. Tap Hatterene and she'll talk to you.
- **Today's stock:** 6 random items for the run, paid in Tickets.
- **Upgrade tabs:** three tabs, 🟠 Offense, 🔵 Defense and 🟢 Utility. Each card shows its icon with the level in the corner, the name, what it does, and the price: stones of its colour + Tickets. Tap a card for a bigger view with the **UPGRADE** button.
- **Prices:** level n costs n stones, plus Tickets that go up each level.
- **They're temporary:** these upgrades last until the run ends.

## 👻 Death Deals
- **Earning Soul Stones:** when a run ends (left, fainted or finished), you get 🟣 Soul Stones for how well it went:
  - the deepest floor you reached
  - +3 per boss beaten
  - +1 per 4 correct answers
  - +1 per 3 unspent stones
  - any Soul Stones you dug up
  - +2 for leaving with rewards
- **The shop:** the ghostly Hatterene then opens **Death Deals**. It has the same upgrades, but levels bought here are **permanent**, and every run starts with them.
- **Stacking:** Upgrade Shop levels go on top of these during a run and are lost when it ends. They never carry over.
- **Spending later:** you can also visit the Death Deals from the Mystery Dungeon setup screen to spend saved Soul Stones.

## All 25 upgrades
**🟠 Offense**
- **Expert Schooling** (max Lv 20): super-effective hits +3% per level.
- **Punchier Fists** (max Lv 20): physical moves +2% per level.
- **Focused Mind** (max Lv 20): special moves +2% per level.
- **Opening Strike** (max Lv 10): each Pokémon’s first attack on a floor +5% per level.
- **Sharp Eye** (max Lv 10): critical-hit zone on the timing bar 5% wider per level.
- **Charge Up** (max Lv 10): +5 Stamina for the team when a battle starts, per level.
- **Finisher** (max Lv 10): +4% per level against foes under 30% HP.
- **Boss Breaker** (max Lv 10): +3% per level against bosses, Totems and Alphas.

**🔵 Defense**
- **Weathered Warriors** (max Lv 10): environment damage and hazards can’t take you below 1 + level HP (Lv 1 = 2 HP).
- **Rough Skin** (max Lv 5): a foe next to you that hits you with a physical move takes damage equal to the level.
- **Thick Hide** (max Lv 20): 1.5% less damage taken per level.
- **Guard Training** (max Lv 10): Guard blocks 2% more per level.
- **Second Breath** (max Lv 10): heal 2% max HP per level when a battle ends.
- **Evasion Drills** (max Lv 10): +1% dodge per level.
- **Sure Footing** (max Lv 5): 20% chance per level to step over a hazard unhurt.
- **Stairwell Rest** (max Lv 10): stairs heal +5% HP per level (on top of 20%).

**🟢 Utility**
- **Shinier Patches** (max Lv 5): Lv 1 brighter sparkles → Lv 2 sparkle much more often → Lv 3 little craters → Lv 4 a little light → Lv 5 on the minimap too.
- **Deeper Digs** (max Lv 3): dig spots can be dug at least 2 + level times (up to 5).
- **Prospector** (max Lv 5): +1 dig spot per floor per level.
- **Lucky Finds** (max Lv 10): foes drop stones 5% more often per level.
- **Ticket Magnet** (max Lv 10): +5% Tickets per level.
- **Bargain Hunter** (max Lv 10): shop items 4% cheaper per level.
- **Light Bearer** (max Lv 3): in dark sublayers, see 2 + level tiles around your team.
- **Scout’s Map** (max Lv 3): Lv 1 stairs on the minimap → Lv 2 dig spots too → Lv 3 items too.
- **Forager** (max Lv 5): 8% chance per level to find an item on each new floor.
