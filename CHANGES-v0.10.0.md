# v0.10.0: Costume variants
- Costumes are a variant, rolled alongside shiny in the Safari: 1 in 40 per Poké Ball (1 in 20 with a Deluxe ticket). A Pokémon can be shiny and costumed at once.
- Variant sparkles: yellow = shiny, cyan = costume, crimson = shiny costume (Box bubbles and the stat sheet).
- Seasonal costumes like Pokémon GO events (original pixel art in tools/make_costumes.py): Witch Hat (Oct), Winter Beanie (Nov–Feb), Flower Crown (Mar–Apr), Graduation Cap (May–Jun, Sep), Sun Hat (Jul–Aug).
- The hat follows the Pokémon frame by frame while it wanders, using SpriteCollab's head positions (tools/make_costume_anchors.py adds them to the sprite manifest).
- Rare Candies: new LEVEL UP button on the stat sheet (1 candy = straight to the next level). Daily-shop TMs now cost Rare Candies (Basic 1, Disc 2) instead of Cogs.
