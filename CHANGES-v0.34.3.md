# PokémonCollege v0.34.3
- **Spread moves:** Surf, Dazzling Gleam, Sludge Wave, Earthquake, Discharge, Rock Slide, Heat Wave, Blizzard, Icy Wind, Snarl, Swift, Razor Leaf, Bubble, Powder Snow, Struggle Bug, Incinerate and Brutal Swing now hit every opposing Pokémon at once. Each target takes 75%, as in the main games.
  - The move animation plays on every target together.
  - No target picking is needed.
  - Enemies using these moves hit your whole team. Dodge, Guard and the Diamond shield each apply per Pokémon.
- **Undo a completed task:** tap the green ✓ on a task in the Done section to move it back to your list. Everything that task paid out is taken back: Cogs (including any early bonus), its Poképuff, its EXP, the Rare Candy if it was your 8th task, and your companion's EXP from it.
