# PokemonCollege v0.63.1

## Tougher dungeon foes
| | Easy | Balanced | Hard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extra levels for foes that can still evolve | +4 | +7 | +10 |
| Foe damage (attack multiplier) | ×1.15 | ×1.35 | ×1.55 |

- **Unevolved foes are stronger:** wild Pokémon that can still evolve spawn that many levels above the normal cap. The first stage of a three-stage line gets 1.5× the bonus. Bosses keep their own scaling.
- **Every wild foe hits harder:** the attack multiplier applies to all of them, on top of the per-difficulty limit on how much one hit can take (0.5× / 0.65× / 1.2×).
- Applies to Mystery Dungeon and the classic dungeon. Tower Battle is unchanged.
