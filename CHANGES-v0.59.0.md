# PokémonCollege v0.59.0

## ⛏ Mystery Dungeon: cave layers
- **8 layers, 152 floors.** The dungeon now digs downward through the planet. Each layer has a set number of floors and is split into **sublayers**.
- **Sublayers:** a sublayer lasts 1–3 floors and has its own effect on battles, plus its own look: floor colours, props, obstacles and wall decorations. Similar sublayers share some art.
- **Digging cutscene:** plays when you start a run and whenever a new sublayer begins. It doesn't play between floors of the same sublayer.
  - A slice of the planet: 8 coloured bands around a glowing core, based on your picture.
  - A tunnel digs down to the new sublayer and a flag pops up at the end with a puff of dust.
  - A white line runs from the flag to the layer's name (larger, black outline) and "Layer N — Sublayer" below it. Both glitch into place.
  - The view irises out on the dig site to black, then irises back in on the floor.
  - **Tap anywhere to skip.**
- **Round button (top left of the floor):** shows the sublayer's icon and the floor. Tap it for the layer, the sublayer, what its effect does and how many floors it has left.
- **What sublayer effects can do:**
  - Make some move types hit harder or softer.
  - Make some Pokémon types tougher.
  - Chip damage after each round. It never knocks out your team.
  - Heal your team or give it Stamina after each round.
  - Make it dark, so you only see close by.
  - Change how far your team moves.
  - Give extra Tickets or items.
  - Make some wild Pokémon types more common.
  - Make multi-hit moves hit once.
  - **Obstacles:** stalagmites, pillars, coral, kelp and more block movement and attacks. They never wall off a room, the stairs or an item.
  - **Hazards:** brine, geysers, acid, lava and falling stalactites cost a little HP when you end a move on them. Some types are immune, and it's never your last HP.
- **Bosses:** still guard the stairs every few floors as before. The last floor (B152F, The Final Seal) always has a boss. Beating it ends the run at the core.
- **Runs in progress:** continue in a sublayer picked for the floor you're on.

## 🗼 Battle Tower environments
- **Can't knock you out:** environment damage (Sludge Hell, Crystal Mines, King's Battlefield) never takes your Pokémon below 1 HP. At 1 HP they take nothing.
- **Banner and damage numbers:** when the environment acts, a banner says so ("The sludge bubbles up…") and the damage numbers show on each Pokémon. Holy Sanctum's healing shows the same way.
- **Round button (top left):** shows the environment's icon and the floor. Tap it for the environment's description and which floors it lasts.

## 🎨 New icons
- Almost every emoji in the app is now a soft, glossy painted icon, in the style of the reference you sent.
  - **Menus:** minigame icons, the Backpack, the Study Minigames header.
  - **Currencies:** Cogs, Tickets, Poképuffs and Rare Candies.
  - **Battles and the rest of the app:** items, environments and the other emoji. Text symbols like ✓, ✗, ★ and arrows stay as text.
- **Minigame icons:** Mystery Dungeon (a cave entrance), Battle Tower, Luxor (a marble ring around a pyramid), Flashcards, Match-Up and Speed Round (a stopwatch with a bolt) each have their own drawing.
- **Safari:**
  - Safari Ticket, Shiny Safari Ticket and Deluxe Safari Ticket each have their own ticket.
  - "The tall grass is rustling…" shows three painted tufts of grass that sway.
- **TMs in the shop:** each shows a TM disc in its move's type colour. Same disc for every rarity.
- **Settings buttons:** now use a silver gear, so they don't look like the gold Cog currency.

## Fixes
- **Frenzy** is now a ★★★★★ Legendary card. Its card art had been left showing 3 stars.
- **Leaving Magearna's shop:** the menu you tap now opens straight away. Magearna's goodbye cry still plays.
- **Box:** the backpack badge on Pokémon that are on a run is a small corner icon again (it had grown to fill the whole circle).
