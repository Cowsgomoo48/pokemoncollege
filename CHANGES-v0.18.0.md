# v0.18.0: Minigames (part 1)
- New Minigames menu: a big Mystery Dungeon button (coming next) and four games: Luxor, Flashcards, Match-Up, Speed Round.
- Every game uses your summaries (key terms + AI quiz questions) and one of three study modes:
  - Smart Study: covers everything once (unseen first), then leans on your weak spots.
  - Learn: repeats each item until you get it right twice; finishing marks the set "learned".
  - Review Weak Points: only items you've missed; unlocks once Learn is finished on a set.
- Your answers are tracked per item, so weak spots carry over between games.
- Make-Up questions: in Luxor (and later the Dungeon), a wrong answer shows the explanation and then a Make-Up question on the same idea asked the other way round.
- Luxor: drag to aim, release to fire type-coloured marbles; match 3+ to break them, combos chain. The chain speeds up over time. Every N seconds (default 15, adjustable 5–60) a question pauses the game: right = big push back + slow-down, Make-Up right = smaller push, both wrong = speed-up. Your main Pokémon sits in a bubble under the slingshot holding the next marble and tosses it up after each shot.
- Flashcards: tap to flip, "Got it" / "Still learning".
- Match-Up: 3 rounds of up to 6 terms and meanings to pair, timed, misses counted.
- Speed Round: 60/90/120 seconds of quick questions with a streak multiplier.
- Finishing any game: 5 Cogs + 15 EXP. The old single quiz is replaced (PokéPics' Quiz button opens Flashcards for that set).
