# v0.73.0 — Study Hall, Nursery, genders

## New facilities (unlock tickets in the Box pool, tier 3)
- **Study Hall 📚**: the Pokémon inside earns EXP for every fully correct answer. Higher levels give more EXP and add Cogs every 10 answers.
- **Nursery 🥚**: pick a ♀ mother and a ♂ father; lines lead to the egg box, which shows Shiny chance, Rarity bucket, Species chance and the father's moves.
  - Parents must share a type. Legendaries and babies can't breed. Ditto stands in for either parent (two Ditto can't breed).
  - Species: mother's line 70% / father's line 30%. Hatches as the first stage.
  - Each father move (Fast / Charged) has a 30% chance to pass down.
  - Shiny parents boost shiny odds (×4 one, ×8 both). Ditto ×1.5. A costumed parent gives extra costume rolls.
  - 6 h to breed + 6 h to hatch (shorter with levels). Each 100%-correct answer makes the timer run 1% faster (stacks; stronger with levels).
  - Breeding Pokémon are locked like run Pokémon and show a 🥚 on their Box icon.
- **Genders**: ♂/♀ shown in the Box (genderless and legendary Pokémon have none).
