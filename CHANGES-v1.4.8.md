# v1.4.8

- **Victory screen** (Slay the Spire style). After a won fight, a Victory panel lists the rewards as buttons; tap each one to collect it:
  - 🎟️ Tickets (including any event bonus Tickets)
  - Relics from Elites and event fights
  - The dropped potion. If both slots are full, you get the replace-or-leave choice.
  - 👑 Choose a Boss Relic (boss floors)
  - 🃏 Add a card to your deck. This opens the card choice; BACK returns to the list.
- EXP, Rare Candy, eggs and Traveler's Biscuit are still given automatically and listed under the rewards.
- The bottom button reads **SKIP** while rewards are left (they're forfeited) and **PROCEED** once everything is collected.
- The victory question now comes after you leave the victory screen, not the instant the fight ends.
- Reopening the app mid-rewards brings you back to the victory screen with the same rewards.
- Service worker cache bumped to 1035.
