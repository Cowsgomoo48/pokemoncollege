# v0.85.0 — Misc tab: Pokédex + Advancements
- New ☰ Misc tab (far right of the dock) with two big buttons: Pokédex and Advancements. A pulsing ! shows on the tab and the button when an advancement can be claimed.
- Pokédex: every species in the app by number; uncaught ones show ?. Tap a caught one for types, base stats (with total) and its animations (Idle/Walk/Attack/Sleep… with ↻ Turn).
- Advancements (68): Study, “Logging” In, Pokémon, Dungeon. Tiered ones (I–V) share one counter and are joined by lines; a higher tier can only be claimed after the one before it.
  - Board: drag to pan, pinch (or mouse wheel) to zoom. Tap a square for icon, name, description, progress and rewards; tap the rewards to claim. CLAIM ALL at the top when anything is ready. Claimed squares get a ✓; a reward box slides up with the rewards flowing in.
  - Entering the board after unlocking something plays a short cutscene per new advancement (camera moves to it, grey square shakes, grows and slams into colour). Tap to skip.
  - Rewards: Cogs + main-companion EXP, plus items (EXP Tickets, Hastening Tickets, Facility Tickets) and random eggs on harder ones. Shiny and costume advancements include a Facility Ticket.
  - Past progress counts where the app already had data (correct answers, re-answered misses, finished tasks and early finishes, Pokémon owned, shinies, costumes, unlocked Ultimates, equipped signature moves). Everything else counts from this update.
- New Pokédex icon.
