# PokémonCollege v0.60.3

## Mystery Dungeon
- **Labels on top of the map:** the floor label, the round sublayer button, the minimap and the message bar stay solid.
- **When a Pokémon is underneath one:** it turns see-through and taps go straight through to the map. This works for your team and wild Pokémon alike.
- **When the Pokémon moves away:** it's solid and tappable again.
- **Timing:** this is worked out from where the camera is heading, so it switches at the right moment while the view slides or zooms.
