# v0.22.0: Soft item art
- All 26 item and decor sprites redrawn in a soft, glossy painted style (gradient shading, coloured outlines, highlights, light spots and a pastel rainbow sheen): snacks, toys, small and medium decor, tickets and boxes.
- Items are now smooth-scaled instead of pixel-scaled, so they stay crisp at any size in the bag, shop, habitat and tray.
- The art is generated by tools/make_items_soft.py; the old pixel versions can still be rebuilt with tools/make_items.py.

# v0.22.1
- Decor (9 small + 3 medium) redrawn as detailed pixel art matching the shop background: cel shading, outlines in a dark tone of each object's own colour, warm palette. Generated by tools/make_decor_pixel.py and shown pixel-crisp.
- Snacks, toys, tickets and boxes keep the soft painted style.
