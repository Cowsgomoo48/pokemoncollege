# v0.27.0: Legendary Ultimates
- Every Pokémon in the Legendary pool (21) has an Ultimate: a third ability with Power 180 that costs the full Stamina Bar (100).
- Unlock it in the Box for 25 Rare Candy (Box → Pokémon → "UNLOCK · 25 🍬"). Once unlocked it shows under the Basic and Disc moves as ✦ ULTIMATE with its effect.
- In the Mystery Dungeon, after answering FIGHT's question, a ✦ Ultimate button appears next to the two moves (lit only with a full bar). Single-target Ultimates ask for a target; then the attack bar runs as usual (Celebi's Time Warp skips the bar and only works on a correct answer).
- Splash screen: your torn black band winks in, the Pokémon's head slides down toward its left edge while the move name scrawls across, then the band winks out and the Pokémon's own animation plays (orbs, tidal wave, earth spikes, beams, lightning chains, drills, shadow punches, hex nuts, cages, X-slash and more).
- Ultimates: Mew Genetic Implosion · Celebi Time Warp · Regirock Earthen Barricade · Regice Permafrost · Registeel Corkscrew Cannon · Kyogre Origin Pulse · Jirachi Doom Desire · Genesect Techno Blast · Xurkitree Overload Surge · Celesteela Orbital Strike · Magearna Fleur Cannon · Marshadow Spectral Thief · Meltan Hex Nut Swarm · Melmetal Double Iron Bash · Regieleki Thunder Cage · Regidrago Dragon Energy · Slither Wing Ancient Wingbeat · Iron Moth Ion Flare · Chi-Yu Ruination Blaze · Iron Valiant Mirror Edge · Ogerpon Ivy Cudgel (effects as designed in chat; each shows in the Box).
- New battle states these use: per-enemy paralysis/flinch (skips its next attack), Thunder Cage damage over time, Melt (+15% damage taken), team damage boosts (stack with Overclock), Earthen Barricade (−10% damage next enemy turn), Permafrost (slower attack bar next turn), Celesteela in orbit (untargetable for one enemy turn), Doom Desire landing at the start of your next turn. Damage over time can win a fight on the enemy's turn.
