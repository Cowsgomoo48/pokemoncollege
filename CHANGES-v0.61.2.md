# PokémonCollege v0.61.2

## Mystery Dungeon upgrade balance (from the test runs)
- **Thick Hide now works:** its damage cut was applied before the game limits how hard a foe can hit, so most hits ignored it. It now applies after that limit, and to boss blasts too, so 20% at max really means 20% less.
- **Offense bonuses add together:** they used to multiply each other, which roughly doubled damage when everything was maxed. Now Punchier Fists / Focused Mind, Expert Schooling, Opening Strike, Finisher and Boss Breaker are added up. Example: a maxed physical, super-effective first hit is +80% instead of about +120%, and most hits land around +40–50%.
- **Sharp Eye:** 2% wider critical-hit zone per level (was 3%), so 20% at max.
