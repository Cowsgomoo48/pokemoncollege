# PokémonCollege v0.40.2

## Shiny Lure and shiny or costumed box Pokémon are much rarer
- **Shiny Lure:** weight 0.5 → 0.05 (Rare tier), about 10× rarer.
  - Per box pull: PokeBox 0.05%, GreaterBox 0.07%, UltraBox 0.08%.
  - Per Daily Deal item slot: 0.04%.
- **Shiny Pokémon (box prize):** weight 0.12 → 0.015 (Legendary tier), about 8× rarer.
  - Per box pull: PokeBox 0.007%, GreaterBox 0.015%, UltraBox 0.03%.
- **Costumed Pokémon (box prize):** weight 0.12 → 0.02 (Legendary tier), about 6× rarer.
  - Per box pull: PokeBox 0.01%, GreaterBox 0.02%, UltraBox 0.04%.
- **How the odds rank:** a random shiny Pokémon is now a little rarer than a Shiny Ticket (0.02, which lets you pick the Pokémon). A costumed Pokémon is about as rare as a Costume Ticket (0.03).
