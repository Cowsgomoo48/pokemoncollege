# PokémonCollege v0.40.1

## Shiny and Costume Tickets are much rarer
- **Shiny Ticket:** weight 0.2 → 0.02 (Legendary tier). That's about 10× rarer:
  - Per box pull: PokeBox 0.01%, GreaterBox 0.02%, UltraBox 0.04%.
  - Per Daily Deal item slot: 0.01%.
- **Costume Ticket:** moved from Rare to Legendary tier, with weight 0.35 → 0.03. That's about 10–25× rarer:
  - Per box pull: PokeBox 0.014%, GreaterBox 0.03%, UltraBox 0.06%.
  - Per Daily Deal item slot: 0.015%.
- **Unchanged:** Shiny Lure (triple shiny odds for 3 Safaris), the box prizes "Shiny Pokémon" and "Costumed Pokémon", and every other weight.
