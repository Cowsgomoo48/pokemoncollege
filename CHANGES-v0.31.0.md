## New Ultimates
- **Groudon: Precipice Blades** (Ground, all foes): a roar, a hop, a wide slam, then a row of earthen blades rushes across the field from behind Groudon.
- **Regigigas: Display of Power** (Normal, all foes): Regigigas slams down, lifts a mountain, hurls it into the sky, and a much larger one crashes onto the foes. On your next turn your team takes 2 less damage from every attack (minimum 0) and restores 6 Stamina once, and the attack bar moves 10% slower. Regigigas rests that turn.
- **Diancie: Diamond Storm** (Rock, all foes, never super effective): Diancie rises and gathers diamonds, then a diagonal storm of diamonds rains down. Your team takes no damage during that enemy turn, under a diamond shield that fades once the enemies finish.
