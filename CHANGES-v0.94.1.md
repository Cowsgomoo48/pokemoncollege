# v0.94.1: Tap to Harvest
- Tap a planted card and press HARVEST to trigger its Harvest effect. This works once per turn, costs no AP and asks no question. You can Harvest on the same turn you plant the card.
- Planted cards whose Harvest you haven't used this turn have a pulsing green border. The buttons are HARVEST first, then REAP when the card has one.
- When you press END TURN, any planted card you didn't Harvest Harvests automatically. Then every planted card loses 1 counter (Lurking Ivy gains 1 instead), and cards that reach 0 counters are discarded.
