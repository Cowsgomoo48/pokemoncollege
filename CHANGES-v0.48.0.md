# PokémonCollege v0.48.0

## 🗼 Battle Tower is back, on the classic battle screen
- **What it is:** battles back to back, one floor after another, with no map. It uses the battle screen from the old Mystery Dungeon (v0.45): foes along the top, your team along the bottom, the same attack animations, questions, timing slider, items, Guard and Ultimates. Bring up to 4 Pokémon. Find it in Minigames, under Mystery Dungeon.
- **Climbing between floors:** when you clear a floor, the camera zooms out to show the tower with your team standing on its floor. Your team rides up to the next floor, a banner says what's waiting there (a battle, a 👑 boss, a 🛒 shop or a new environment), then the camera zooms back in on the new battlefield. Tap to skip.
- **Foes:**
  - Normal battles have 1–4 foes, with more and stronger ones as you climb.
  - Swarms (15% chance after floor 2) bring 4–6 weaker copies of one Pokémon.
  - Every 10th floor is a boss: a normal boss, a Totem (floor 10+) or an Alpha that attacks twice (floor 30+). Beating a Totem or Alpha gives you its pre-evolution.
- **🛒 Shop floors:** one floor in every 10 (somewhere from its 3rd to 8th floor) is a Kecleon Shop instead of a battle. You pay with this run's Tickets.
- **Loot:**
  - Tickets: 3+ from every battle, more from swarms and bosses.
  - Battle items: 30% chance per battle (45% from swarms). Bosses always drop 2.
  - Rare Candy: 30% chance from bosses.
- **Boss challenges:** before each boss you can accept an optional challenge or skip it. Completing it doubles that boss's Tickets and adds a rare-or-better item and a Rare Candy. The four challenges are:
  - Win without using an item.
  - Answer 4 questions right on the first try.
  - Win without anyone fainting.
  - Win within 6 rounds.
- **Perks:** every 5 floors you choose 1 of 3. There's no other healing between battles besides items.
  - 🍲 Hot Meal: heal everyone 50%; fainted Pokémon come back with 25%.
  - 💫 Revival Herb: fainted Pokémon come back with 50%.
  - 💪 Power Charm: +10% damage.
  - 🛡️ Iron Shell: take 10% less damage (up to 30%).
  - 🔋 Battery Pack: +20 Stamina at the start of each battle.
  - 🍀 Lucky Ticket: +25% Tickets.
  - 📦 Supply Drop: 2 random items.
- **Environments:** every 10 floors brings a new environment with its own battlefield background, in a shuffled order each run. Environment effects apply to the foes too.
  - ⚡ Electric Workshop: Electric moves +30%.
  - 🌿 Grass Jungle: Grass moves +30%.
  - 🌸 Misty World: Fairy moves +30%.
  - 🔮 Psychic Mindscape: Psychic moves +30%.
  - 🏙️ Concrete Jungle: Grass and Rock moves +15%.
  - 🐾 Vicious Wilds: multi-hit moves hit only once.
- **Records:** the Tower saves your top floor, bosses beaten, challenges won, runs and total floors cleared.
- **Run end:** leftover Tickets become Cogs 1:1, and you earn EXP for the floors you cleared.
- **One run each:** you can have a Mystery Dungeon run and a Battle Tower run going at the same time, but a Pokémon can only be on one of them.

## Small changes
- The shop is now called the Kecleon Shop in both modes.
- Mystery Dungeon still plays on the tile floors from v0.47.0.
