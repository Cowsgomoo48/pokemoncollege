# PokemonCollege v0.62.1

## Move animations
- **Bug (weak):** the attacker lunges forward while a pair of white fanged jaws opens over the target and snaps shut. The bite comes with a pink burst behind it and yellow sparkles. (Replaces the needle shot.)
- **Water (strong):** steam leaks from under the target's feet while the ground darkens and they shake. Then a geyser erupts from below and throws them upward, and the hit lands on the way up before they fall back down. The geyser is see-through, so you can watch the target rise. (Replaces the whirlpool.)
