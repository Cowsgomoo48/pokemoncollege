# v0.13.1
- Size now matters in the pasture: a Pokémon is drawn at its own size, from XXXS (0.5×) to XXXL (2×).
- New size scale: XXXS ≤0.6×, XXS ≤0.7×, XS ≤0.82×, normal, XL ≥1.25×, XXL ≥1.45×, XXXL ≥1.75× (max 2×). New pulls use a bell curve: XS/XL about 1 in 12–16, XXS/XXL about 1 in 120–150, XXXS about 1 in 3,400, XXXL about 1 in 10,700. Pokémon you already have keep their sizes.
- Removed the single-pose Gardevoir "Midnight Coat" costume that slipped into 0.13.0 (you'd said no thanks). The code that supports Rare Costumes stays, with none defined.
