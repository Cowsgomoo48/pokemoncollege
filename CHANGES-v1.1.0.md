# v1.1.0

## Multi-type cards
- New: dual-type cards. They have a half-and-half border and show both type names.
- They can appear as rewards, in shops and in transforms when your team has either type.
- Type bonus: every Pokémon with either type counts once.
- Type-based relics (Prism Badge, Chimera Emblem) see both types.
- 20 dual-type cards: Dead Drop, False Bottom, Swarm Harvest, Pollinate, Votive Shield, Keystone Wish, Spirit Courier, Gravewind, Bitter Medicine, Antivenom, Arc Flash, Heat Sink, Read the Current, Undertow Memory, Punish the Opening, Dirty Counter, Deep Reserve, Carapace Mold, Glacial Fault, Ashen Debt.

## New Abilities (19)
Afterburn, Riptide, Closed Circuit, Seed Bank, Permafrost, Second Stance, Combat Rhythm, Quarantine, Excavation Site, Trail Markers, Glide Path, Echo Theatre, Swarm Tactics, Heavy Footing, Afterlife Clause, Ominous Presence, Paradigm, Assembly Line, Wishweaver.

## Rulings
- **Permafrost** triggers once per enemy per enemy turn, so in practice every attack that uses up Chill. The Vulnerable lasts through your next turn.
- **Riptide**, **Closed Circuit**, **Combat Rhythm**, **Swarm Tactics**, **Trail Markers**, **Heavy Footing** and **Ominous Presence** scale with copies, like the other Abilities. Riptide's scaling is how many discards per turn go on top.
- **Ashen Debt** removes as much Burn as it can, up to 3.
- **Dirty Counter's** option only appears when you play it in an enemy's Haste window before an attack.
- **Paradigm** and **Echo Theatre** free plays don't ask a study question. Paradigm can't take Abilities, Plant cards or Constructs.
- **Wishweaver:** if Ward triggers during the enemy turn, you choose from the cards that were in your hand at that moment, after your next draw. If none of them are still in your hand, you choose from your full hand.
