# PokémonCollege v0.44.2

## Workout Gym: about 2 months to max growth
- **Breakthroughs:** about 1 in 5 Gym hits (20%) is a **breakthrough** that raises a random growth rate by one grade. Every other hit is a "good workout" that still pays training EXP and still counts toward the 3 hits before lights-out.
- **Unchanged:** the hourly check (1% start, doubling on each miss), the half-rate EXP and the 6-hour sleep.
- **Time to max all six growth rates (simulated, Gym starting at Lv 1):**
  - Typical Pokémon (growth around C to C+): about 2 months (46–78 days, average 62).
  - Pokémon starting at all D-: about 3 months (average 95 days).
