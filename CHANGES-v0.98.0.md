# v0.98.0 — Elite rooms

- New 😈 Elite rooms on the tower map (from the 4th floor up to 2 floors below the boss). Every tower of 8+ floors has at least one.
- Elites are Mythical Pokémon, each with its own rule (tap the icon under its name to read it):
  - Marshadow — Spectral Thief: +2 Strength whenever you play a non-Attack card.
  - Jirachi — Deep Sleep: sleeps up to 3 turns gaining Block; hurting it wakes it. Charges Doom Desire every 3rd turn.
  - Genesect — Drives: Burn, Chill, Shock, Douse Drive attacks in turn, then Techno Blast.
  - Melmetal (with 2 Meltan) — Double Iron Bash gains 1 hit every time.
  - Celebi — Time Travel: every 3rd turn rewinds its HP to 2 turns ago.
  - Manaphy — Heart Swap: every 3rd turn swaps its debuffs with your team's.
  - Diancie — Diamond Shell: starts with 30 Block that doesn't run out.
  - Volcanion — Steam Jets: your team takes 2 damage per Attack card played (can't KO).
- Elite rewards: extra Tickets, a Relic, EXP, and a card choice. No repeat Elite until you've met them all.
