# v0.77.0 — Egg Clause
- Pokémon hatched from eggs release for 10% of the usual Cogs and Poképuffs (single and Mass Release).
- Unhatched bred eggs also release for 10% (Totem/Alpha eggs keep full value).
- Hatched Pokémon show a small egg in the Box corner; shinies show their ✦ on top of it.
- Applies to Pokémon hatched from now on (earlier hatches are not marked).
