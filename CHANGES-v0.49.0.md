# PokémonCollege v0.49.0

## 📊 Export Study Data
- **Where:** Summaries → 📊 STUDY DATA (all sets), or open a summary → 📊 EXPORT STUDY DATA (just that set).
- **What it makes:** a plain-text report you can paste into a chat with Claude or another tutor. For each study set it lists:
  - how many terms, quiz questions and practice problem types the set has, how many you've practiced and when you last studied;
  - total answers and % right, plus a right/wrong tally for each of your last 10 study days;
  - **Struggling with:** your most-missed items, each with right, wrong, right on the first try, right on a Make-Up and how often you used hints;
  - **What I picked when I got it wrong:** the actual question, the answer you chose and the correct answer, for your 3 most recent misses on each item. For practice problems this includes the exact numbers, so a tutor can spot the mistake;
  - what you're doing well on, and what you haven't practiced yet.
- **The sheet starts with a short note for the tutor** explaining what the numbers mean.
- **📋 Copy** puts it on the clipboard. **💾 Save File** saves a .md file to Files → On My iPhone → PokémonCollege → Study Data.
- **Already counted:** right/wrong totals from before this update are included. First try, Make-Up, hints, the wrong answers you picked and the daily tally start recording from this update on.

## 🗼 Six new Battle Tower environments
- 🕊️ **Holy Sanctum:** at the end of your turn, your Pokémon heal 3 HP.
- ☣️ **Sludge Hell:** after both sides have moved, the floor bubbles: 1 damage to every Pokémon (yours and the foes) that isn't Poison or Steel type.
- 🐉 **Dragon Cavern:** Dragon-type Pokémon take 10% less damage from all attacks.
- 🏛️ **Legend's Temple:** Legendary Pokémon take 5% less damage and deal 10% more damage. "Legendary" means the app's Legendary rarity (this includes Mew, Jirachi, Ultra Beasts like Xurkitree, and similar).
- 🌊 **Trepidation Habitat:** Water-type attackers deal extra damage equal to 5% of their own max HP to each target they hit.
- 🌙 **Sleepless Hollow:** items can't be used. The "no items" boss challenge doesn't show up here.
- **For both sides:** like the first six, these effects apply to foes too (except Holy Sanctum, which only heals your team).
- **Backgrounds:** each has its own battlefield background.
- **Runs in progress:** a Tower run that's already going adds the new environments after the six it already has.
