# PokémonCollege v0.46.2

## Battle Tower set aside for now
- **What's removed:** the Battle Tower mode from v0.46.0. Minigames shows Mystery Dungeon, Luxor, Flashcards, Match-Up and Speed Round as before.
- **Mystery Dungeon:** it's back to how it played in v0.45.1. The Battle Tower update had also added traps, events, rescues and hidden stairs to Mystery Dungeon, and those are set aside too.
- **Saved for later:** everything from that build (tower.js, the tower backgrounds, the reworked dungeon files, the full v0.46.1 zip and its changelog) is kept outside the app in `pc_shelved/battle_tower_v0.46.0/`, ready to bring back.

## Kept
- **Evolutions:** still cost half as much: 5 / 10 / 20 Rare Candies, and 75 for the most expensive.
- **Everything from v0.45.1 and earlier:** unchanged, including Dungeon Tickets, TM rerolls, Facilities and the attack animation fix.
