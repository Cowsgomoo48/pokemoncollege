# v1.6.3: Exam 3 practice problems

Six new practice problem types (Physics), each with a step-by-step walkthrough and fresh numbers every time.

| Type | Problems |
|---|---|
| **Rotational kinetic energy & moment of inertia** | K_rot = ½Iω²; I for sphere (⅖MR²), cylinder (½MR²) and hoop (MR²); ω from stored energy; which shape stores the most |
| **Rolling without slipping** | Speed at the bottom of a ramp (mgh = ½mv² + ½Iω²); which shape wins a race; what fraction of the energy is spinning; v_cm = ωR |
| **Rotational kinematics** | ω = ω₀ + αt, θ = ω₀t + ½αt², ω² = ω₀² + 2αθ, α = Δω/Δt |
| **Linear momentum & impulse** | p = mv, FΔt = Δp (bouncing ball), recoil |
| **Collisions** | Perfectly inelastic, elastic with a target at rest, KE lost, what's conserved |
| **Center of mass** | 2–3 masses on a line |

- **Equation choice:** practice and "Help me start" have two new equation groups, **Rotation & Rolling** (7 equations) and **Momentum & Collisions** (4 equations). Problems mix the equations within each group.
- **Auto-detection:** these types turn on automatically for study sets that mention these topics.
  - Sets where you ticked types by hand need the new ones ticked in the summary editor.
