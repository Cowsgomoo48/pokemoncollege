# v0.74.0
- **Landfill 🗜️** (new facility, Box tier 3): needs a Pokémon to run. In the Press menu, drag (or tap) bag items onto the press; they join the pile and are crushed one at a time (10 min each at Lv 1, 5% faster per level).
  - Each item fills the gauge by rarity: 8 / 15 / 30 / 60% (× level bonus). A full gauge makes a random ticket or box.
  - Pokémon EXP: +600 × (rarity+1) per item crushed, +15,000 per thing made.
  - Tap a queued item to take it back. The press pauses with no Pokémon.
- Hastening Tickets now also work on Nursery egg hatching.
