# v0.82.0 — Manaphy rework
- Overlapping specials after the first dive: while underwater, Manaphy has a 50% chance to add a Rain Dance or Drizzle Beam on top of its Hydro Pump / Water Balloons (beam only while its ripple is visible). While dazed it may also call Rain Dance (40%). All still hit 1 turn after they are shown.
- Yank Protection: each yank adds a counter; after diving, Manaphy shows no ripple for that many turns.
- Rain Dance aims about 40% of its drops on or next to your team.
- Below 25% HP Manaphy dives deeper: digging it out takes 4–6 questions (was 2–5) and the daze after a yank is 1 turn shorter (minimum 1).
