# v0.72.0 — Precalculus practice problems

New generated practice types (random numbers, app-checked answers, step-by-step hints):
- **Function transformations:** where a point (a, b) lands on y = c·f(kx + m) + v.
- **Function notation in context:** f(A + 2) vs f(A) + 2 vs 2f(A) vs f(2A), in paint, temperature and ticket-cost settings.
- **Even and odd functions:** classify polynomials, find the matching symmetric point, combine even/odd functions.
- **Unit circle:** find sin θ from cos θ (or the reverse) with the quadrant sign, using 3–4–5, 5–12–13, 8–15–17, 7–24–25 and 20–21–29 triangles.
- **Angle identities:** sin/cos of 180° ± θ, 360° − θ, −θ and 90° − θ (in degrees or radians).
- **Sine and cosine graphs:** amplitude, period (in terms of π) and midline.
- **Ferris wheels:** amplitude, midline, the h(t) = D − A·cos(2πt/P) model, and height at a time.
- **Radians and arc length:** satellite orbits (adding altitude to Earth's radius), clock hands (exact π answers), degrees to radians, s = rθ.
- **True or false:** "Which statement is true?" about trig, log and symmetry rules.

Extended:
- **Logarithms:** now also asks log-rule questions (product/quotient/power) and log equations like ln(kx − k) − ln(x − 1) = ln x.
- **Exponential equations:** now also two-base equations like 3(1.2)ˣ = 2(1.3)ˣ.

Summaries pick these up automatically from their notes. A summary whose AI guide already saved a practice list keeps that list until it's rebuilt.
