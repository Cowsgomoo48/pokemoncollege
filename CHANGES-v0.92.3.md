# v0.92.3: Tower card balance
- Heavy Blow: 8 → 11 damage.
- Dominating Chords: 3 → 1 AP; 1 → 3 HP per trigger.
- Pressure: 3 → 2 AP.
- Doubling Season: base cost 8 → 5 (still −1 per card drawn this turn after the opening hand).
- Plant cards gain 1 counter at the start AND end of each of your turns (still capped at X).
- Healing from cards gets the type bonus (+15% per matching team member, same rate as Block); shown live on the card. Ability triggers (Dominating Chords) still don't.
- Enemy HP in deck battles: ×1.5 → ×1.25 (base AP stays 3).
