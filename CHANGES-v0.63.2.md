# PokemonCollege v0.63.2

## Learned % on summaries
- Each summary in the Summaries list shows a **"__% learned"** badge next to its title: grey under 40%, yellow from 40%, green from 80%.
- It's worked out from your study data: the share of that set's questions you've actually learned.
- A question counts as learned once you've answered it right at least twice, you have no recent misses on it, and your right answers outnumber wrong ones at least 2 to 1.
- Questions you've never seen, or keep missing, don't count yet, so the badge only goes up as you really learn the set.

## Gemini notices
- The notices about Gemini (switching from one Flash model to another when one is used up, and "about __% of today's free limit left") now disappear after about 2.5 seconds instead of 5. Tapping one still dismisses it right away.
