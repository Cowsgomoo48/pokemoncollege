# v1.0.1 — Balance: early Elites, signature bosses, boss relics

## Elites (Melmetal, Marshadow, Genesect, Diancie)
- All Elites now respect the floor gates on their type moves (no Close Combat, Stone Edge, Moonblast, etc. before floor 5). Before, Elites used every move from floor 4.
- Melmetal: Double Iron Bash 0.40 → 0.35 × attack per hit; hit cap 4 → 3; Meltan attack 0.8 → 0.5 × the floor's enemy attack. The cool-down after each Bash is unchanged.
- Marshadow: Spectral Thief +2 → +1 Strength per non-Attack card.
- Genesect: Drive attacks 0.8 → 0.7 × attack; Techno Blast 1.9 → 1.6 × attack.
- Diancie: no number changes (only the floor gates).

## Signature bosses (HP × and attack × on top of the normal boss formula)
- Aggron: HP 1.35 → 1.1, attack 1.0 → 0.86.
- Gardevoir: HP 1.45 → 1.05, attack 1.55 → 1.45, Future Sight 2.4 → 2.3 × attack.
- Tinkaton: HP 1.35 → 0.9, attack 1.35 → 1.2, Gigaton Hammer 1.1 → 1.05 × attack per hit.
- Volcarona: HP 1.4 → 1.1, attack 1.6 → 1.45, light attacks 0.7 → 0.75, Fiery Sweep 0.55 → 0.5 per hit, disruption threshold 10 → 12 × (1 + floor/40) (14 on floor 8).
- All counterplay unchanged (Block chip, countdown, recovery turn, threshold).

## Boss relics
- Sealed Purse: +1 AP now only on turns 1, 3, 5… (Ticket penalty unchanged).
- Overloaded Battery and Glass Crown unchanged.
- Tuning switches live in DC_BR / DC_ET / DC_HB in tools/deck.js.
