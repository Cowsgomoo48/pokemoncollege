# v1.2.0: Equation Lab

New minigame: **🧪 Equation Lab** (Minigames menu). It practises the step word problems trip you up on: choosing which equation to use.

## Modes
- **⚡ Pick the Equation:** read the problem and pick the right equation. This is the fastest way to get lots of reps.
- **🧾 Set It Up:** tap each quantity as *given* or *asked for*, then pick the equation.
- **🧮 Full Solve:** set it up, pick the equation, then choose the answer. Every problem ends with the worked solution.
- **♾ Endless:** keeps going, with your weak equations coming up more often.

## How it teaches
- **Inventory step:** checks hidden numbers, like "from rest" (v₀ = 0), "until it stops" (v = 0) and "dropped" (a = g).
- **Wrong equation:** if you pick the wrong one, you see when to use the one you picked and when to use the right one. For kinematics, it also tells you which quantity is missing.
- **Hints:** a 💡 hint on every step. It doesn't give the answer away.
- **Mix-up log:** the end screen lists your mix-ups ("picked X when you needed Y"). Every equation tracks right, wrong and what you confuse it with. **Study Weak Spots** drills only those equations.
- **📘 Equation Cards:** a reference card for each equation: the formula, when to use it, and your record.

## Content
29 equations and 46 problem types. Numbers are random, and the answers are worked out in code.
- **Physics:** Kinematics, Forces, Work/Energy/Power, Projectiles, Circular Motion, Circuits.
- **Precalculus:** Arcs & Angular Speed, Sinusoidal Models, Unit Circle Identities.
- **Algebra:** Growth & Interest.

Topics that match practice types in your study sets are pre-selected.

## Rewards
Same as other minigames, plus +1 Cog per 4 problems in Full Solve.
