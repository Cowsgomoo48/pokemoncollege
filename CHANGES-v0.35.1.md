# PokémonCollege v0.35.1
- **Reread All** (PokéPics): reads every photo in the current view again. On the home screen that is every folder, including those inside groups. Inside a group it is just that group's folders. Summaries rebuild afterwards.
- **Select** (PokéPics): tap folders and groups to pick several, then Move them into a group (or a new one) or Delete them. Deleting a group deletes the folders inside it.
