# PokemonCollege v0.62.5

## New Rare Costume: Navigator
- For **Braixen** and **Delphox**, with full animated sheets (walk, idle, attack, sleep, and eat for Braixen), copied as-is from your pack.
- It stays on when Braixen evolves into Delphox.
- Like the other rare costumes, a wild one has a 1 in 2048 chance to be wearing it.
- No shiny version yet: shiny Braixen and Delphox wear the normal Navigator colours until a shiny set is added. Then just drop it in and remove `noShiny`.
- Shiny fallback (any rare costume): if a costume has no shiny sheets, the normal ones load instead of the sprite failing.
- Credits files are kept with the sprites.
