# v0.15.0: Rare Costume — Sage (Charcadet, Armarouge, Ceruledge)
- Your sheets, bundled as-is (walk, idle, attack, sleep; Charcadet also eats). Shiny + costume uses your shiny sheets (cyan code; Armarouge's white jacket). Credits files kept with the sprites.
- Rolls at 1/2048 on any Charcadet, Armarouge or Ceruledge from the Safari. A costumed Charcadet keeps Sage whichever way it evolves.

# v0.15.1
- Big companions (like an XXXL Gyarados) no longer poke out of the top of the habitat: a Pokémon never walks higher than its own height allows, in both the normal and enlarged habitat.

# v0.15.2
- Size range is now XXXS 0.5× to XXXL 1.5×. Cutoffs: XS ≤0.82, XXS ≤0.7, XXXS ≤0.6; XL ≥1.18, XXL ≥1.32, XXXL ≥1.45 (max 1.5). Anything rolled above 1.5× before this update is shown at 1.5× in the habitat.

# v0.15.3
- New app icon (your Magearna graduation art), at full 1024px for the iPhone home screen; the build workflow now uses resources/icon-1024.png.

# v0.15.4
- Stats start at the base stat: at Lv 1 every stat equals the game base stat ÷ 10 (± nature). Each level adds a little on top: Stat = B + B × (Lv − 1) × BV × (1 + AV) ÷ 10.

# v0.15.5
- HP has its own formula on the app scale: HP = B + (Lv − 1) × (2B × (BV + AV) + 1) ÷ 10 (± nature), still equal to the base at Lv 1.

# v0.15.6
- A single tap opens the big habitat (✕ closes it); a tap still wakes a sleeping Pokémon first.
- Double-tap zoom is turned off across the whole app.
