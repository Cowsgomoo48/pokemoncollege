# PokémonCollege v0.52.0

## 🏰 Mystery Dungeon: free roam
- **No foes in sight, no turns:** while no wild Pokémon is visible, moving doesn't use up anyone's turn. Keep tapping tiles and walk as far as you like without pressing End Turn.
- **Longer steps:** each free-roam step can go up to 6 tiles (more for Pokémon fast enough to move farther in battle). After each step the move tiles come back up, so exploring is just tap, tap, tap.
- **Your team follows:** the rest of the team (fainted Pokémon too) walks behind whoever you're moving, so you don't have to move each one.
- **Resting while you walk:** each free-roam step heals your team 3% HP (at least 1).
- **When a foe shows up:** the step that brings a wild Pokémon into view counts as that Pokémon's move, and normal turns start. Your other Pokémon still have their full turn.
- **Free roam on screen:** the move button turns blue, and the panel says "No wild Pokémon in sight: walk freely."
