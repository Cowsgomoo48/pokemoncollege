# PokemonCollege v0.62.9

- **Locked during combat:** while a battle is on, Kecleon Shops, Treasure rooms and dig spots are greyed out and can't be entered or dug. Walking onto one does nothing, and they stay where they are.
- They come back as soon as free movement returns. If you're already standing on one when the fight ends, step off and back on to enter it.
- Resting rooms are unchanged.
