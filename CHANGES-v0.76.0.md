# v0.76.0 — Nursery nests and eggs
- 3 nests on the right of the Nursery: 1 at Lv 1, 2 at Lv 4, 3 at Lv 6. Tap a nest, pick an egg into the confirm slot (shows rarity, source, likely growth, shiny chance, inherited-move chance, hatch time), START HATCHING. Hatching eggs cannot be removed.
- Breeding now leaves an egg in the Bag (new Eggs tab). Tap an egg for its menu: release it for Cogs (3/6/12/25/50 by rarity) or put it in a nest. Eggs can't go in the Landfill.
- Totems and Alphas give eggs instead of Pokémon (boosted growth and size kept; 1/4096+5% shiny rolled on hatch).
- Hatch times: Common 1 h, Uncommon 1.5 h, Rare 2 h, Very Rare 3 h, Legendary 6 h (Nursery levels shorten them 5%/level as before).
- Each fully correct answer adds +1% (more with levels) to breeding and to every egg currently hatching; each egg keeps its own bonus.
- Hastening Tickets can target breeding or any hatching nest.
- Old in-progress Nursery eggs move into Nest 1.
