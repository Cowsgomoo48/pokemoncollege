# v0.97.0 — ? Room events reworked

- New multi-step events with character art:
  - **The Great Meowscarada**: Volunteer (1 card becomes 2 random cards of its type), Pick a card (relic, upgraded card, or 2 Daze), Toss 15 🎟️ for a relic.
  - **The Brew Stall (Gardevoir)**: Healing Draught, Strength Tonic, Fizzy Elixir (apply to your next fight), and a free Mystery Concoction.
  - **The Fortune Teller (Cinderace)**: Moon, Star, Sun, or pay 5 🎟️ to choose what every room on the next floor becomes.
  - **Two Meloetta**: a small blessing, or a bigger reward plus a Scorch.
  - **Mountain of Coins (Gholdengo)**, **The Unown Archive**, **Spinda's Wheel**, **Sleeping Snorlax**, **Kecleon's Swap Meet**, **Shedinja's Bargain**, **Double or Nothing (Alakazam)**.
- No repeat events until you've seen them all. Ambush chance is now 15%.
- Event fights can pay bonus rewards.
- New test: tests/tower-events-browser.cjs.
