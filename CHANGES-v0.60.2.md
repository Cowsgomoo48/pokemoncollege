# PokémonCollege v0.60.2

## Mystery Dungeon fixes
- **Kecleon Shops (and chests, springs and the boss-cleared screen):**
  - These screens now scroll, so every item can be reached.
  - **LEAVE** (or the action buttons) stays stuck to the bottom of the screen, always visible.
- **Battle camera:**
  - **Where it centres:** on the middle of the line between your Pokémon and the awake foe that are furthest apart. It's worked out again every time something moves, so it follows the fight as Pokémon move, faint or join.
  - **Zoom:** when the two wouldn't both fit on screen, it zooms out (down to 62%) so the whole fight is in view. Tapping tiles still works at any zoom.
  - **After the battle:** it goes back to your lead Pokémon at full size.
