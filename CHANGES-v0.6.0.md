# v0.6.0
- Sutodo: one branch per due day (other sorts: one per 3 tasks), compact bubbles under it, spacing between days grows with the gap in due dates (capped). Branches render behind the body. Smaller branches.
- Wandering companion: name tag sits right under the sprite's feet (measured from the sheet).
- Each Pokémon has its own level (Box bubble badge + sheet). The player level stays on the home screen. Rule: your ★ companion earns the same EXP you do (100 per level, max 100); HP grows 2 per level. Existing Pokémon start at Lv 1.
- Safari grass animation cropped (dark border removed).
- Magneton sprites are now bundled; any other species missing from the bundle loads from SpriteCollab when online; otherwise the portrait shows.
- Falinks removed (existing ones are archived like other retired species).
- Box sheet overhaul: ✕ back to Box, name with ✎, type/weight/height, variant sparkles by height (yellow = shiny), HP from stats, six-stat hexagon (game base ÷ 10) with 6 random growth letters (F=.01 … A+=.14), nature ±1 on the final stat (▲▲ green / ▼▼ red), move colour = type, green ▲ = STAB, level bar under companion time.
- Pokémon EXP: each finished task gives the ★ main 4,537.5 EXP (half an hour of companion time), +10% per full day left before the due date, plus 9,075 EXP per hour as main (Lv 100 after about 90 days from time alone).
- Level curve: EXP from level L to L+1 = 0.8 x L^3.
- Fully offline: portraits (114) and species data (types, weight, height, base stats from Pokémon Showdown) are bundled; no network requests remain. The service worker precaches every sheet and portrait for the web version.
