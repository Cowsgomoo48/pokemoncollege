# v0.97.1 — Enemy movesets

- Enemies now use moves from their own types (all 18 types, about 6 moves each). Dual types mix both lists.
- Intents show the move name; "X used Move!" when it acts.
- New enemy actions: attacks with side effects (Burn, Weakened, Vulnerable, Enfeebled, Toxin, status cards, self Strength/Block/heal), self-heals, debuff-only moves, and charge moves (Solar Beam, Dig, Sky Attack, Charge → Thunderbolt) that gain Block, then hit hard next turn.
- Stronger moves unlock on later floors; bosses can use every move and favor the strongest ones.
- Ward and Simple Beam also stop debuffs that come with attacks.
