# PokémonCollege v0.59.1

## Mystery Dungeon fixes
- **Fainting:**
  - A fainted wild Pokémon now staggers, plays its sleep animation and fades away, instead of vanishing.
  - Your own fainted Pokémon stagger and then lie asleep until they're revived.
- **Moving after a battle:** once no awake wild Pokémon is in sight, the battle ends right away. Everyone can move freely again, even teammates that already moved or acted. Sleeping Pokémon in view no longer stop free roam. Attacking one wakes it up.
- **Using items:** after you pick an item to use on a teammate, the item list goes away so the whole team is in view. Your teammates are highlighted in green, and a **CANCEL** button takes you back to the item list.
- **Camera:**
  - When a battle starts, the camera centres on the middle of the room and stays there, instead of jumping between Pokémon. In a corridor it centres between your team and the foes.
  - When the battle ends, it goes back to your lead Pokémon.
