# v0.19.0: Mystery Dungeon
- Minigames → Mystery Dungeon. Pick 1–4 Box Pokémon (your main can go if you choose a new main), confirm, then pick the study sets the questions come from, then a difficulty:
  - Easy: 1 floor, enemies a little above your team's average level (about 15–20 min).
  - Balanced: 2 floors, slightly tougher, better treasure, Totem fights possible (about 25–30 min).
  - Hard: 3 floors, tougher still, Totems and Alphas possible (about 45–60 min).
- The map fills the top of the screen (Slay-the-Spire style paths); the bottom shows study time, room number and your run bag. Every floor ends in a boss.
- Rooms: Battles (1–4 enemies), Swarms (4–7 weaker enemies of one species), Treasure (a few questions; more right = better loot), Rest (+10 Energy and 20% max HP), and Shops (Apple, Oran Berry, Starf Berry, Overclock, Time Gear, bought with Cogs).
- Before a boss on Balanced/Hard you may be offered a Totem (strong evolution + 2–3 pre-evolution allies) or, on Hard, an Alpha (attacks twice a turn). You choose whether to take it. Beating one gives you its pre-evolution for your Box: +5% shiny chance, no F growth, and a 25% chance of one extra size step (smaller for Totems, bigger for Alphas).
- Battles: your team runs in from the bottom. On each Pokémon's turn:
  - FIGHT: answer a question (Hint available), pick the basic or TM move, tap a target, then stop the timing slider (0.5× at the edges up to 1.25× near the middle, crit dead centre). The attack always lands. A first-try answer makes the slider slow and the crit zone wide, a Make-Up answer makes it a bit harder, and missing both makes it much harder. With the slider turned off in dungeon settings, the damage multiplier uses your weighted table instead.
  - ITEM: a crosshair in the Pokémon's colour (blue, red, green, yellow) marks the target, then a question. First try = used. Make-Up = 25% fumble (item kept, no effect). Both wrong = item lost, no effect.
  - GUARD: 2 questions. Perfect = 75% less damage and Energy based on Speed (min +10); Make-Up = 50% (min +6); fail = 25% (min +4).
  - Fainted Pokémon skip their turn, but can answer a question to come back at 1 HP.
- HP and Energy carry over between rooms. Rewards: Cogs per win, 4,537.5 EXP per Pokémon (×1.5 Swarms, ×3 bosses), bosses can drop items and Rare Candies, and clearing the dungeon gives a bonus (30/60/120 Cogs).
- Save & Exit at any time and continue later from the Minigames menu. Pokémon on a run show a 🎒 badge in the Box and can't be released, evolved, given new moves or made main until the run ends.
