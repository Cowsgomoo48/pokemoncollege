# v0.96.3: Madame Isfrid

## Look and layout
- The merchant is now **Madame Isfrid**, framed close up in the shop scene.
- She has 4 expressions: neutral, pleased, content and sigh. Her backgrounds were removed, keeping the sparkles and the sigh lines. Each expression is a WebP of about 85 KB.
- Her lines appear in a speech bubble in the top-left of the scene.

## When each expression shows
- **Neutral:** her idle pose. Other expressions return to neutral after about 2.5 seconds.
- **Pleased:** after a purchase under 15 🎟️, when you leave, and on some taps.
- **Content:** after a purchase of 15 🎟️ or more.
- **Sigh:** when you try to buy without enough Tickets, or tap her 5 times within 4 seconds.

## Lines
- **First visit ever:** "Madame Isfrid, at your service."
- **Later visits:** "Welcome back, spooks."
- **Leaving** (the room closes after her line): "Ta ta, spooks." / "See you soon." / "Have fun out there!"
- **Buying:** "Ooh, a fine choice~" / "Pleasure doing business, spooks." / "Treat it well~"
- **Big purchase:** "Now that's what I like to see~" / "Oh my, a big spender."
- **Card removal:** "Snip! Gone without a trace~"
- **Not enough Tickets:** "You're a little short, spooks…" / "Tickets first, darling."
- **Tapping her:** "Hmm? Something catch your eye?" / "Browse all you like, spooks." / "Everything here is one of a kind~"
- **Tapped too many times:** "I'm not for sale, spooks…"

## Buttons
- BUY buttons are no longer greyed out when you're short on Tickets. Pressing one triggers her sigh instead.
