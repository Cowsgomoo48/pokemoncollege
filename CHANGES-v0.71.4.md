# v0.71.4 — Eternabeam phases

- The beam's three sizes are now its three damage phases:
  - **Small beam:** deals the first third of the pool, 1 HP every 0.1 s.
  - **Medium beam:** grows with a shake and deals the second third, 1 HP every 0.03 s.
  - **Giant crackling laser:** deals the final third, 1 HP every 0.01 s.
- Each phase deals its damage while it's active and holds briefly before growing.
- Damage numbers now draw on top of the laser.
