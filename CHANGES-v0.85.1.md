# v0.85.1
- Nursery nests only show in the Nursery tab (no longer on every facility).
- Opening the Nursery no longer pops up the parents menu by itself.
- Legendary Pokémon can breed and fill either the mother or father slot; two Legendaries can't pair.
- Answerer and Second Wind reset to zero (one time): rewards already claimed from them are taken back, and they count from this update.
- The Bag can't be opened on the Misc tab (Pokédex / Advancements).
