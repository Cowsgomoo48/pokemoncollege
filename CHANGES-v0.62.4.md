# PokemonCollege v0.62.4

- **Resting Room over the floor:** in Mystery Dungeon, the Resting Room now opens on top of the current dungeon floor instead of replacing it. The floor stays visible behind the campfire art, dimmed and slightly blurred, with a warm glow in the middle. The floor badge and minimap sit underneath the dimming too.
- LEAVE closes the overlay and returns you to the same spot on the floor.
