# v0.78.0 — Dungeon vistas (visual only)
- Some walls open onto deep backgrounds that scroll slower than the map (parallax), tinted per dungeon layer (sea glow, lava glow, golden core...).
- Mine shafts: openings beside rooms show timber frames, lanterns, rails and a mine cart far below.
- Bridges: long straight corridors can become rope bridges or railways over chasms (rock spires, mist; water or lava far below in sea/molten layers).
- Huge rooms can overlook ancient ruins (columns, arches) or abandoned wooden houses deep below.
- Roughly: ~60% of floors get a mine shaft, ~40% a bridge, ~17% a ruins/houses vista. Movement, combat and the minimap are unchanged.
- Fixed: Box sort helper mirrored into tools/box_sort.js (box-sort test).
