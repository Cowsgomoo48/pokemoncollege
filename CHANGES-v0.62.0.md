# PokemonCollege v0.62.0

## Allow Halfsies? (run setting)
- New checkbox in Mystery Dungeon / Tower **Run Settings** (the gear during a run). You can switch it on or off mid-run.
- While it's on, a typed answer that's at least 50% of the right answer (by letters, against the answer and its accepted alternatives) gets **🍰 Halfsies!**
  - It counts as a hit in battle (damage, turn and so on).
  - It's still recorded as a miss: it goes into your stats, the recap and your weak spots, and the full answer and walkthrough still show.
  - No make-up question for a Halfsies answer. The button is CONTINUE.
- Dig spots: a Halfsies answer gives the half (make-up) reward and doesn't cave in the spot.

## Dig spots are manual now
- Walking onto a dig spot no longer starts digging.
- Out of battle, when the selected Pokémon is standing on an undug spot, **⚔️ FIGHT is replaced by ⛏ DIG** (shows how many layers are dug). Tap it to dig.

## Computer-course study guides
- When an AI summary is built for a set whose title or notes mention "computer" or "comp" (Comp Sci, COMP 210 and so on), it uses a separate builder. That builder focuses on:
  - exact commands and syntax in backticks
  - what each option does
  - "situation → exact command" examples with awkward file names
  - "What command would you type to …?" questions with realistic near-miss wrong answers
  - the command-line practice problems
